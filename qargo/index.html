Sound familiar?

Signs your Qargo setup is working against you

These come up again and again after go-live. None of them means you chose the wrong system. They mean the setup has not caught up with how you work.

  • Jobs go into Qargo, but the real planning still happens on a whiteboard, a spreadsheet or in someone's head.
  • Rate cards and fuel surcharge do not price every job correctly, so invoices get checked and changed by hand.
  • Updates to a pallet network or a customer do not always go through, so someone keys them in again.
  • The people trained at go-live have moved on, and new starters learn from whoever sits next to them.
  • Each problem gets its own support ticket, but nobody has looked at how the whole setup fits your operation.
